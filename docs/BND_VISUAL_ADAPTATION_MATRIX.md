## 2. Must Keep

- controlled asymmetry;
- one dominant focal point per scene;
- stable modular frames;
- concentrated cyan hierarchy;
- dark layered surfaces;
- semantic routes and endpoints;
- central objects with distinct business roles;
- technical microcopy as support;
- alternating density;
- readable whitespace;
- honest pending/preview states;
- native HTML/CSS/SVG controls;
- responsive recomposition;
- reduced-motion completeness;
- full-page continuity.

## 3. Must Not Copy

- text, logos and legal claims from references;
- unverified metrics, cases, testimonials and percentages;
- exact generated material detail;
- malformed/duplicated raster text;
- unreadable microcopy;
- random symbols and fake charts;
- literal dimensions without responsive adaptation;
- raster controls;
- uniform cyan flood;
- decorative lines with no role;
- fake system uptime;
- game-HUD spectacle;
- desktop layout scaled down to mobile;
- exact font imitation through `scaleX()` or tracking hacks.
