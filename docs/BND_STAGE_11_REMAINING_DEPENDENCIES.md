# BND.STUDIO — Stage 11 Remaining Dependencies

## Purpose

This document separates the work that can be completed during Stage 11A from the work that still requires unavailable production or runtime dependencies.

## Requires Blender

- replace the current Hero media proxy with the approved `BND AI CORE` render;
- calibrate the final cube silhouette, camera, crop, physical mass and surface response;
- tune final local light, material contrast and render-sequence transitions;
- validate the closed and unfolded cube states against the approved Hero composition.

Target substage: `Stage 11B — Blender Hero Integration & Hero Recalibration`.

## Requires live interaction review

- subjective transition timing for all six Problem Explorer scenarios;
- subjective transition timing for all four Product Assembler directions;
- focus preservation in a real browser session;
- live keyboard traversal and repeated state switching;
- mobile interaction feel and intermediate layout-shift inspection;
- final Stage 8 transition approval.

Target substage: `Stage 11C — Live Interaction Review & Final State Calibration`.

## Requires real content

- approved project cases, screenshots and results;
- final quantity and structure of Projects materials;
- final production copy lengths;
- final factual proof points and client evidence;
- final display-font decision, if a licensed or separately approved font is selected.

Target substage: `Stage 11D — Production Content, Contact and Final Full-Page Approval`.

## Requires production contacts

- approved Telegram handle;
- approved production email address;
- decision on the primary contact channel;
- final form scope and response expectation;
- final contact microcopy.

No placeholder address may be promoted as production data.

## Requires backend or legal review

- real form submission;
- server action or external endpoint;
- persistence and delivery logic;
- consent text and privacy wording;
- production success and error states;
- retention policy and final privacy route approval.

## Explicit Stage 11A boundary

Stage 11A must not simulate or close any dependency listed above. Its valid endpoint is:

`STAGE 11A — COMPLETED / AWAITING FULL-PAGE VISUAL APPROVAL`

It is not equivalent to final website approval and does not authorize Stage 11B–11D automatically.
